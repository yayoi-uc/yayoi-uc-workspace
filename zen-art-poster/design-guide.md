# zen art poster - Design Guide

## Visual Direction
Reference: Karimoku Case (https://karimoku-case.com)

## Layout Principles
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Margins: generous (min 80px on all sides)
- Text alignment: left-aligned (avoid center alignment except cover)
- Max text area: 60% of slide width
- Photos: full-bleed or large cropped, no borders/shadows
- One idea per slide - do not crowd
- No bullet points where possible - use short paragraphs or single lines

## Photography Style
- Natural light, soft shadows
- Warm tones (slightly desaturated)
- Interior/lifestyle context (linen, wood, ceramics alongside kakejiku)
- Overhead or straight-on angles
- Reference: Kinfolk, Cereal Magazine aesthetic

## Do's and Don'ts
- DO: Use whitespace generously
- DO: Let photos speak
- DO: Keep text minimal and precise
- DON'T: Use gradients, drop shadows, or effects
- DON'T: Use more than 2 colors per slide
- DON'T: Fill every space - emptiness IS the design
